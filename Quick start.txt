CLICKTRACK
Metronome, song tempo finder, and click-track WAV exporter

OPEN
Windows: extract the entire ZIP into a folder you can write to, then open
app/ClickTrack.exe. Keep the EXE and _internal support folder together.
Mac: choose arm64 for Apple Silicon or x86_64 for Intel (macOS 15 or newer).
Extract the ZIP and drag ClickTrack.app to Applications, then open it.
The Mac app is not notarized. If blocked after attempting to open it, use
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway to approve your download.
See https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445 for Apple's first-launch instructions.
No Python installation is required on either platform.

SET YOUR OWN TEMPO
Type a BPM between 20 and 300, use the slider, or tap T to a steady beat.
Press Start click. Space plays and pauses playback.
Choose beats per bar, subdivisions, an accented first beat, sound, and volume.
Changing a setting pauses playback. Press Play to hear the new settings.

FIND A SONG'S BPM
Choose a song or drop audio files onto the window. Common formats include
MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, WMA, and AIFF. Songs can be up to 30 minutes.
The estimated BPM is applied automatically. Enable 'Play song with the click'
to check the result. Adjust 'First click at' to move the clicks against the song.
The half-speed and double-speed buttons help with ambiguous rhythms.
The detector estimates one steady tempo. Live performances, tempo changes,
quiet introductions, syncopation, and unusual rhythms can need manual correction.
The first click is an estimated pulse; a song's bar/downbeat is not identified.

YOUR SONG LIBRARY
Click Add songs to choose one or several songs. New songs are analyzed once
and added to the saved library. Click any song in the list to instantly load
its saved BPM, beats per bar, subdivision, sound, volume, and first-click offset.
Press Start click when you are ready to play. Songs are not played automatically
when selected. Any adjustments save automatically with the selected song.
Drag songs to arrange a setlist, or select one and use Up / Down. Rename changes
its library title. Remove removes only its library entry and keeps the song file.
Your library and ordering are remembered when you close and reopen ClickTrack.
The original audio stays in its current folder; ClickTrack stores a reference.
If a file moves, its saved click settings still work. Select the song and use
Locate audio file to reconnect it. Selecting an existing song uses your saved
tempo corrections, without analyzing it again.
Windows saves library.json and settings.ini in data beside the app folder.
Mac saves them in ~/Library/Application Support/ClickTrack. Back up this data
to keep your setlist and practice marks; back up your song files separately.

SONG POSITION AND PRACTICE LOOPS
The timeline below Start click lets you choose where a song or its click track
starts. Click or drag the playhead, then press Play. While playing, dragging
restarts at the chosen position when you release the mouse.
Snap to beat is on by default. The playhead and A/B brackets snap to the song's
main beats using its saved BPM and first-click offset. The left/right arrows
step by one beat. Turn Snap off to type an exact position to a millisecond.
Pause keeps your current position, so Play resumes from that spot.

Drag the A and B brackets to mark a practice section. Set A and Set B place
the brackets at the current position; the A/B time fields also allow editing.
Loop section repeats the range from A up to B. Song audio and the click repeat
together; the click keeps the original beat and bar accents. Uncheck Loop
section to play onward. Enable Play song with the click to hear the recording.
Loop marks are remembered separately for each library song on this computer.
Beat snapping follows the estimated BPM; adjust BPM and First click at if the
song's timing is different. Recordings with changing tempo can still drift.
Exported WAVs still contain the full selected length, without the practice loop.

SAVE A CLICK TRACK
Choose a length, then Save WAV. The exported WAV contains only the click.
With a song imported, 'Match the imported song's length & offset' uses its
duration and the First click at setting. For a custom length, uncheck that box;
the first click then starts at time zero. Export uses 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM.
The original song stays separate. Click volume also sets the exported volume.
Custom exports may be up to two hours.

AUDIO OUTPUT
Use the system default output or choose an output in the app. If you connect new
headphones, press Refresh. Bluetooth may add audible delay to visual feedback.
Song playback uses a lower song level to leave room for the click.

PRIVACY AND SUPPORT
Song analysis and playback run on this computer. Files are not uploaded.
No account, cloud service, subscription, or API key is required.
The error log is clicktrack.log in the data folder described above.
Windows decoder scratch files are in work/temp beside app; Mac uses cache/temp
inside the user data folder. Third-party licenses are in licenses.
Editable source and build recipes: https://github.com/Dr-Ake/Click-Track
