The Qt, PySide, Shiboken, PortAudio and libsndfile libraries remain dynamically
linked in app/_internal on Windows or the .app Contents/Frameworks on Mac
and can be replaced by compatible builds. Re-sign a modified Mac app locally
with codesign --force --deep --sign - ClickTrack.app. Their LGPL
terms permit reverse engineering to debug modifications to these libraries.

component. Compile a replacement using the included exact source and recipe;
